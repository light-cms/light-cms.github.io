Why should you care about writing, and will people actually read your work in today's world?

While not everyone will read what you write, some will. Think of it this way: if you spend two or three sleepless nights fixing a problem and documenting the solution, you might save someone else from losing their own sleep.

You might wonder why not use a platform like Medium, which offers great distribution. The simple answer is that you lose control. On Medium, you often submit your work to publications, meaning it is entirely up to editors whether or not to publish it. Furthermore, many of these platforms place paywalls in front of your content, and exporting your articles later can be a major headache.
