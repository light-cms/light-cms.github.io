#### Why start a Blog?

We have talked about sharing ideas, experiences, and whatnot, but do people care about what you write? And do people read yours? The answer is a big no. Everyone can’t be a target audience. If your target audience is everyone, then you end up writing for no one.

For example, A problem you have solved by wasting 10- 15 hours of your life on research and browsing 10 different articles or documentation sites,  and if you want to make life easier for someone else who is currently in your shoes, then that person is your ideal target audience. Even if you haven’t done something that worked but took a lot of digging. you can leave trails to make life easier for others. The person whom you have saved a sleepless night will remember and dig into your posts.

#### How to start a blog ?

Starting a blog had many options previously. But with the php2shell vulnerability, WordPress, or other PHP-based solutions are no longer an option. And why should your content be fetched from a database?  So using a Static site for personal use cases is more than enough for personal use cases, documentation sites, etc.

Moreover, training LLMs requires a lot of real data. Which your blog or experience is going to feed, resulting in your experience helping 100s and 1000s.
