## How to choose

Choose based on your content workflow, not feature count:

- **Writers need a visual editor:** evaluate LightCMS or TinaCMS. [VERIFY]
- **Developers want schema control:** evaluate Keystatic or Decap CMS. [VERIFY]
- **Teams want minimal setup:** evaluate Pages CMS or Sveltia CMS. [VERIFY]
- **AI-generated changes need review:** evaluate GitCMS. [VERIFY]

Before migrating, confirm front matter compatibility, media paths, authentication model, and build pipeline behavior. Most Git-based CMS migrations are configuration changes, but field names and media handling often need adjustment.
