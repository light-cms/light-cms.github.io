LightCMS and Ghost both support publishing, but they are built for different outcomes. LightCMS is a Git-based workflow for static site content. ## When to choose Ghost

Choose Ghost when memberships, paid subscriptions, native newsletters, and an integrated publishing platform are central to your business model.

## When to choose LightCMS

Choose LightCMS when your site is primarily content and you want static delivery, Markdown in Git, and a simpler operational model. You can add external services for newsletters or memberships, but that requires integration work.

## Trade-off

Ghost bundles publishing and audience monetization features but requires operating or paying for a platform. LightCMS keeps publishing simple and portable but does not replace a full membership platform. The right choice depends on whether the site is a publication or an audience business.
