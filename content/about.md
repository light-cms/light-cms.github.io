**This is the blog of the LightCMS team.**

LightCMS is a Git-backed content workspace for editing and publishing Markdown — a visual editor in front of a repository you own. The product lives at **[lightcms.me](https://www.lightcms.me/)**; the writing lives here.

![The LightCMS workspace: a visual Markdown editor committing to a Git repository](/og-image.webp)

## What We Write About

We publish what we learn building and running LightCMS, plus the wider problems around it:

- **Static sites and generators**: Astro, Hugo, Eleventy, Zola, Jekyll, what each is good at, and how to choose. See [best static site generators](/blog/best-static-site-generators/).
- **Git-backed and headless content**: how file-based publishing differs from a database, and when each wins. See [what is a Git-backed CMS?](/blog/git-based-cms-explained/), [headless vs Git-backed](/blog/headless-vs-git-based-cms/), [what is a headless CMS?](/blog/headless-cms-explained/).
- **The writing workflow**: Markdown, front matter, drafts, review, and rollback. See [what is front matter?](/blog/what-is-front-matter/), [version control for writers](/blog/lightcms-drafts-and-versioning/).
- **Comparisons**: side-by-side write-ups of LightCMS against every CMS people actually evaluate. See [Payload](/comparison/lightcms-vs-payload-cms), [Sanity](/comparison/lightcms-vs-sanity), [Contentful](/comparison/lightcms-vs-contentful), [WordPress](/comparison/lightcms-vs-wordpress), [Decap](/comparison/lightcms-vs-decap-cms), [TinaCMS](/comparison/lightcms-vs-tinacms), and [the full list](/comparison/).
- **Product deep-dives**: the [editor](/blog/lightcms-editor-tour/), the [AI copilot](/blog/lightcms-ai-copilot/), [voice dictation](/blog/lightcms-voice-to-text/), and [starter templates](/blog/start-with-a-lightcms-template/).

## Who We Are

We build LightCMS. We are a small team that got tired of choosing between a comfortable writing experience and content we actually owned. Every post here comes from building the tool, running it on our own sites, and answering questions from people trying to publish without a DevOps job.

We write first-hand. Where we compare other platforms, we say what each one does well and where it wins. The point is helping you choose the right architecture, not winning an argument.
