# LightCMS Journal theme

## Direction

- **Identity:** A warm editorial publication about Git-based publishing and static-site tooling.
- **Audience:** Writers, developers, and teams evaluating content workflows.
- **Personality:** Thoughtful, practical, independent, and calm.
- **Palette:** Parchment and ink in light mode, warm charcoal in dark mode, with one clay accent for links and key actions.
- **Typography:** Native old-style serif faces for editorial content, paired with a quiet system sans serif for navigation and metadata.
- **Mood:** A well-used technical journal rather than a product dashboard.
- **Dials:** ENERGY 2, RHYTHM 2, MOTION 1.

## Decision reasons

- Warm neutrals connect the interface to reading and publishing without imitating paper texture.
- A wide site shell and narrow reading column separate browsing from long-form reading.
- Serif content gives articles an editorial voice; system UI type keeps controls compact and familiar.
- Spacing opens around page transitions but stays denser inside articles to preserve reading flow.
- Article lists use ruled rows instead of cards because dates, titles, and summaries are one continuous archive.
- Gallery items share one flat frame because screenshots have equal importance and need a stable comparison grid.
- Existing product and social icons remain only where they identify a real destination or control.
- The sticky navigation uses the only translucent surface so links stay readable while scrolling.
- Motion is limited to hover, focus, and menu state feedback so reading remains still.
